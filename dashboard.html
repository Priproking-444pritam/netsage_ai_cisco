Applied AI + Network Troubleshooting

Every AI diagnosis waits for a human before it becomes a fix.

NetSage AI reads symptoms and show-command evidence from Packet Tracer labs, proposes a root cause, OSI layer, confidence, next command and fix — then hands it to a reviewer who accepts, edits, or rejects it. Nothing reaches a junior engineer unreviewed.

03 · Responsible AI

When the AI was wrong, and why

Six cases required a human correction. The most serious one is a security catch — the exact reason this workflow never auto-applies a fix.

● flagged case — C030

Guest Wi-Fi reaching internal servers, misdiagnosed as a channel problem

The AI attributed dropped guest sessions to co-channel interference. The real fault was a missing guest VLAN, letting guest traffic fall onto the internal network — a security-boundary failure a performance fix would never have caught.

ai_root_cause: "Wireless channel overlap causing dropped guest sessions"
human_verdict: REJECTED
corrected_cause: "Guest VLAN was never created/trunked — guest traffic falls back onto the native VLAN"

See responsible_ai_log.md for all six corrected cases (C003, C007, C012, C020, C024, C030) with full before/after reasoning.
